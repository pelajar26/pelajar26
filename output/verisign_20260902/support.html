Frequently Asked Questions

The Verisign Customer Center is an online portal for registrars to obtain technical details on implementation, including SDKs, download reports, view announcements, and enroll in marketing programs.

The Verisign Account Center is an online portal for registrars to perform domain name and account management, including contact and user management.

Access to the Verisign Customer Center and Verisign Account Center is restricted to those with Verisign-issued credentials. If you are unable to connect to the Verisign Customer Center, email Support.

Different top-level domains (TLDs) have different requirements and processes and some TLDs require ICANN accreditation. For more information about certification for Verisign-supported TLDs, visit Become a Registrar.

Verisign operates the authoritative registries for .com, .net, .name, .コム, .닷컴, .닷넷, and קום. domain names. Verisign provides registry services to over 2,000 ICANN-accredited retail registrars who, in turn, sell domain names to individual or business end-users.

Search for domain name suggestions or brainstorm ideas using our domain name generator, NameStudio®.

You can register a domain name through participating ICANN-accredited registrars. Visit our Find a Registrar page to get started.

You can register an IDN through participating ICANN-accredited registrars that support IDNs. Visit our Find a Registrar page to get started.

To search for a registrar, visit our Find a Registrar page.

While Verisign operates several domain name registries, we are not a registrar. If your domain name has expired, contact your registrar about renewal options. Registrar information for your domain name can be found in the Whois database.

Visit our Combating DNS Abuse page to learn more about how you can report alleged DNS Abuse.
