Attack Scenario — Step by Step
1
Partner logs into por.etoro.com

Azure AD B2C authentication completes. Angular app initialises with authenticated user object.

2
FullStory activated (50% coin flip)

The app rolls random 1 or 2. If 1, FullStory script is injected from third-party CDN.

Math.floor(Math.random() * 2) + 1 === 1 → FS activated
3
Partner identity sent to FullStory

Partner's internal eToro ID is passed to FullStory, linking the recording to that affiliate account.

window.FS.identify("12345", {})
4
Partner completes KYP form — enters IBAN, bank details, passport data

FullStory has full unrestricted DOM access (no CSP). With older SDK defaults, all keystrokes and form values may be captured.

ibanNumber: "GB29NWBK60161331926819" → recorded
5
Session data transmitted to fullstory.com

All captured DOM events, keystrokes, and potentially form values are sent to FullStory's infrastructure outside eToro's control.

6
Recordings accessible in FullStory dashboard (org: cL5)

Any eToro employee with FullStory access can replay sessions and see unredacted financial data, with no separation from other eToro data.

🔴
GDPR Article 25 Concern Recording sensitive financial data (IBAN, tax IDs) via a third-party service without explicit consent or data minimisation controls may constitute a GDPR Article 25 violation (Privacy by Design).
⚠️
Legacy SDK Risk FullStory SDK v1.2.0 predates the 2023 "Privacy by Default" model. Older snippets may record form inputs unless _fs_settings.privacy.defaultInputMode = "masked" is explicitly set.
Remediation
  • 1
    Add CloudFront + CSP in front of S3. Deploy AWS CloudFront with a Response Headers Policy that sets Content-Security-Policy restricting scripts to 'self' edge.fullstory.com. Also add Strict-Transport-Security, X-Content-Type-Options, and Referrer-Policy.
  • 2
    Mask all sensitive inputs in FullStory. Add fs-exclude or data-private attributes to IBAN, bank account, passport, and TIN fields. Or set SDK default: window._fs_settings = { privacy: { defaultInputMode: 'masked' } }.
  • 3
    Audit FullStory session recordings. Review eToro's FullStory account (org: cL5) to determine whether existing recordings contain unmasked financial data, and purge any that do.
  • 4
    Evaluate session recording scope. Consider whether session recording on KYP/payment data entry screens complies with GDPR Article 25 requirements. Consider disabling recording entirely on financial data screens.
